From an empty repository to a running application with modux: one aggregate (a book) modelled in YAML, validated, generated into a Spring Boot service in hexagonal layers, deployed with its database in Docker, used in the browser — then a new field added through the model, with its migration.

Everything on screen is real: the model files, the output of each mvn modux command, and the generated application running.
