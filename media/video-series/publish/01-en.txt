modux is an open-source, model-driven generator for enterprise systems in Java (DDD, hexagonal architecture, Spring Boot). In three minutes: a concert-ticket system modelled in YAML, generated, deployed and running — then changed through its model.

Everything on screen is real: the modux editor, the output of mvn modux:generate / ai-complete / deploy, the generated code, and the system running. ai-complete is opt-in and uses the AI tool on your own machine. The card gateway is a demo mock.
