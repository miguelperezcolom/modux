How the bounded contexts of a modux system talk to each other, on the real four-context Tickets system: the context map in the editor; Sales and Payments joined by flows, each turned into a Kafka topic with a publisher and an idempotent subscriber; the transactional outbox, switched on with one line per service; a synchronous call from Sales to Catalog's price lookup, over a generated REST client; and a projection that keeps Sales' own copy of every concert, which also gives Sales' forms a picker. Then all of it running: a concert put on sale in Catalog, its copy already in Sales' database, a purchase priced over REST and confirmed once Payments' event comes back through Kafka.

Everything on screen is real: the model files, the code modux 0.6.7 generates from them, and the generated system running in Docker.
